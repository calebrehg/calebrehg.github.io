Privacy Policy

Forewarn Login Helper is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What it accesses: read-only access to the owner's Gmail (the gmail.readonly permission). It searches only for emails from do-not-reply@forewarn.com to find a one-time login code.

What it stores: nothing is collected, shared, sold, or sent to anyone. The Gmail access token and the Forewarn session are stored only on the owner's own computer. Email contents are not saved.
